[ENH] Persona grounding must be first-class in the product model, not a surface-level id

## The problem in plain words

Personas are created — `/shape` writes them as real records under
`product-os/<domain>/*/personas/*` in the same run that cuts the slices. But nothing
downstream is actually handed them.

`/ux` is the case that exposed it. After #548 the UX lens must ground every flow in "a
persona or journey of the hub". That requirement cannot be met:

- `/ux` defines its **hub** as the slice's functionality grounding docs plus the product
  profile. Personas and journeys are not in that definition.
- The contract that hands work to the `author-ux-lens` skill passes slice ref, slice
  record, functionality groundings, profile and product base. There is **no persona or
  journey path**.
- `check_ready_slice.py` does not resolve personas or journeys at all.
- The one thread that does exist is thin: the slice record carries
  `surface[].persona_ref` — the persona who opens each surface. That gives the model a
  persona **id** and nothing else. Not who they are, not what they want, not what they
  are trying to achieve.
- The flow cross-check added in #548 only asserts that a flow names something of type
  persona or journey. It never opens the record to confirm the id resolves, so an
  invented persona passes.

So a requirement was shipped with no input able to satisfy it.

## The wider point — this is the real ask

Persona grounding is not a UX detail. It is how the model knows **who** a thing is for,
and that matters most when the work is driven by a **business intent** rather than a
screen. A business intent is a statement about what someone is trying to achieve; without
a persona behind it, the model is reasoning about an outcome with no one attached to it.

So the fix is not "pass personas into `/ux`". The fix is that personas and journeys become
a first-class grounding source in the product model — resolvable, readable, and carried
wherever intent is reasoned about, the same way functionality grounding docs are today.
`/ux` is the first consumer to need it, not the only one.

## Why this matters

Three things break without it:

1. A flow, an intent, or a decision can name a persona that does not exist, and nothing
   catches it. Grounding that cannot be resolved is not grounding.
2. A persona today is a name. The model cannot read what the person wants, so every
   downstream play re-invents that from context, differently each time.
3. Work driven by business intent has no anchor. The intent says what should be true; the
   persona says for whom. Only one of those is in the model.

## What good looks like

- Personas and journeys are resolvable from the model by id, from anywhere that needs
  them, not just inferable from a slice's surface list.
- A persona record carries enough content to ground a decision: who they are, what they
  are trying to achieve, what they care about, what would make the thing a failure for
  them.
- `/ux` receives the slice's personas and journeys as part of its hub, and its checker
  resolves each persona id against the live model instead of accepting a bare name.
- Whatever reasons about a business intent can name the persona it serves and have that
  reference verified.

## Scope note

Where persona records are authored is a live question. `/shape` creates them today, as a
by-product of cutting slices. Issue #528 proposes a `/story` play that would create
personas and storyboards **before** `/shape`, so the story drives the cut instead of
following it. That decision is not made here — this change is about personas being
first-class **in the model** and resolvable by consumers. Whoever picks it up should read
#528 and draw the boundary explicitly rather than letting the two overlap.

  `product-os/<domain>/*/personas/*`)

  `core/components/memory/standards/schemas/product-os/slice.yaml` (`surface[].persona_ref`)
- `/ux` hub excludes personas: `core/components/plays/ux/reference/ice.md` (C7, and the
  intent's definition of hub)

- the readiness check ignores personas:
  `core/components/plays/ux/scripts/check_ready_slice.py`
- the flow check does not resolve the id:
  `core/components/plays/ux/scripts/validate_ux.py` (the manifest flow grounding walk)
- related, not the same work: issue #528 (`/story` play)
- the defect that surfaced this: issue #548, merged as PR #549
